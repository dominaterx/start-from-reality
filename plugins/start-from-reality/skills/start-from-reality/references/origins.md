# Origins, Adaptation, and Provenance

`Start From Reality` is an original synthesis. It redistributes no book text and is not a substitute for the source works.

## Threat-aware starting

Neil A. Fiore's *The Now Habit* influenced these elements:

- interpret procrastination as a protective response rather than a character verdict;
- restore choice and separate starting from finishing;
- create a failure-recovery safety net;
- protect guilt-free recovery and use completed-work evidence;
- treat persistence as repeated re-entry.

This skill adapts those ideas into shorter, context-sensitive interventions. The usual 5–15 minute entry range and the integrated loop are adaptations, not Fiore's formal scheduling rules.

## Reality-based method

The following method families were distilled from *Selected Works of Mao Tse-tung*, Volumes I–IV only:

| Method family | Representative works consulted | Transfer into this skill |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation before prescription | “Oppose Book Worship”; preface/postscript to *Rural Surveys*; “Reform Our Study” | Gather decisive present facts; do not apply templates mechanically |
| Concrete tension analysis | “On Practice”; “On Contradiction” | Identify stage-specific tensions and test them in practice |
| Staged, adaptive strategy | “A Single Spark Can Start a Prairie Fire”; “Strategic Problems of China's Revolutionary War”; “On Protracted War” | Avoid both fatalism and instant-victory fantasy; protect runway; revise by stage |
| General direction plus concrete case | “Some Questions Concerning Methods of Leadership” | Test a general rule on a representative pilot before scaling |
| Focus with coordination | “Methods of Work of Party Committees”; “On the Establishment of a System of Reports” | Maintain one center while monitoring other essential fronts |
| Material conditions and participation | “Be Concerned with the Well-Being of the Masses, Pay Attention to Methods of Work”; “Get Organized!” | Treat capacity and affected people's knowledge as operational facts |
| Review and error correction | “Talk to the Editorial Staff of the Shansi-Suiyuan Daily”; related review documents | Preserve achievements, locate deviations, and learn through correction |

The source EPUB used during development mixed body text with editorial matter under identical markup. Development therefore excluded Volume V, volume blurbs, recognizable editorial headnotes, and endnote sections. No quotation or textual edition claim relies on that file. The public skill contains paraphrased method structures only.

## Ethical transformation

Ideas developed for revolutionary politics and war cannot be copied straight into learning, personal life, or ordinary organizations. This skill removes enemy construction, coercion, ideological conformity, violence, sacrificial logic, and claims of political authority. It keeps only general reasoning patterns that remain useful under consent, pluralism, reversibility, and personal dignity.

The combined sequence—make action safe enough, examine reality, select the principal tension, run a bounded pilot, inspect evidence, revise and re-enter—is the project's own architecture.
