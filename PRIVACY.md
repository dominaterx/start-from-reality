# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-10

Start From Reality is a skills-only plugin. The plugin itself:

- has no MCP server, app connector, external network dependency, user account, analytics, advertising, or telemetry;
- does not independently collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data; and
- does not require users to provide sensitive personal information.

The ChatGPT or Codex host processes conversation content under the terms and privacy policies that apply to the user's account. Files, tools, or connectors that a user separately enables are governed by their own terms and data practices. Start From Reality does not change those practices.

Users should avoid sharing information they do not want processed by their selected host or enabled tools. This plugin is a productivity aid, not medical or mental-health treatment.

Questions or concerns can be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dominaterx/start-from-reality/issues).
