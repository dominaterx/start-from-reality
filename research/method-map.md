# Method Map and Transformation Record

This development note records how source ideas were converted into a general action system. It contains no source text.

## Source boundary

- Included: Volumes I–IV of *Selected Works of Mao Tse-tung*.
- Excluded: Volume V in full, volume blurbs, recognizable editorial headnotes, and endnote sections.
- Reliability limit: the working EPUB used identical HTML styling for body and editorial matter. It was suitable for thematic discovery, not quotation or textual criticism.
- Output rule: retain only abstract methods, original synthesis, and work-title attribution.

## Transfer decisions

| Source method | Generalizable structure | Personal/project adaptation | Rejected transplant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investigation of concrete conditions | Decisions require relevant present evidence | Inspect the real task, artifact, capacity, attempts, and unknowns | Treating the user as an object; gathering irrelevant biography |
| Contradiction analysis | A stage contains multiple tensions with unequal leverage | Select the actionable tension that unlocks several downstream moves | Labeling people or emotions as enemies |
| Practice and recognition | Models improve through action, observation, and revision | Use bounded pilots and four-line reviews | Claiming one cycle proves a universal truth |
| General guidance plus a particular case | A broad direction needs representative concrete testing | Pilot a study method on one actual exercise or a workflow on one request | Scaling by command before evidence |
| Protracted and staged strategy | Long outcomes depend on changing conditions and intermediate stages | Protect runway; define current-stage exit evidence; revise tactics | Romanticizing sacrifice, warfare, or permanent emergency |
| One center with coordinated fronts | Attention needs a center without losing system awareness | One main task plus maintenance floors | Neglecting health, other deadlines, or dissent |
| Material conditions and lived evidence | Capacity and first-hand knowledge affect execution | Count sleep, food, tools, recovery, and the person's own observations | Using collective rhetoric to override agency |
| Review and correction | Preserve achievements while locating deviations | Compare prediction and result; fix the smallest causal layer | Purges, humiliation, punishment, or total negation |

## Synthesis decision

Fiore's threat-aware model supplies the entrance: a person cannot investigate or practice well while the first move feels like a verdict on their worth. Reality-based methods supply the middle: once contact is possible, collect facts, locate the stage-specific principal tension, and test it. Feedback and re-entry supply the continuation.

The resulting architecture is:

1. make action safe enough;
2. examine present reality;
3. select the principal tension;
4. run the smallest useful pilot;
5. inspect the evidence;
6. revise and re-enter.

This sequence, its routing rules, scoring tests, maintenance-floor pattern, and ethical boundary are project-original choices.
